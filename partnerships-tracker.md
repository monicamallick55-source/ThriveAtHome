## Chapter Launch Partnerships

For each new chapter launch, pursue these local partnerships first:

| Partner Type | Why | How to Find |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| **Local senior center** | Referral source + venue for in-person events | Search "[city] senior center" or contact local Parks & Recreation |
| **Local Area Agency on Aging** | Referral pipeline + credibility + potential contract | aoa.acl.gov/find-help to find your local AAA |
| **Local home care agencies** | Referral source — their clients are your ideal members | Search "[city] home care agency" |
| **Local faith communities** | High trust, senior-dense congregations | Approach through interfaith council or directly |
| **Local employer (500+ employees)** | Employer benefits pilot — fastest B2B revenue | LinkedIn search HR directors at large local employers |

**Bay Area chapter launch partners to target first:**
- San Mateo County Area Agency on Aging (aging.smcgov.org)
- City of San Mateo Senior Services
- Peninsula Volunteers (peninsulavolunteers.org)
- Local senior centers: Foster City, Belmont, San Carlos
